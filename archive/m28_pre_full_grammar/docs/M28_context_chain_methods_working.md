# Context chain annotation Methods working draft

The implemented method separates reference-free candidate generation from a jointly learned context and complete-chain scorer. It targets primary protein-coding CDS exon chains from raw genomic FASTA, rather than complete UTRs or all alternative isoforms. A39 tested an absolute-null and compatible-set objective and closed after failing three of its five TRAIN continuation conditions. A40 tested candidate-role evidence within the ordered chain representation under the unchanged objective and closed after failing two of its five conditions; its full saved-record reconciliation and descriptive attrition are complete. The separate A41 zero-initialized affine correction on frozen A3 emissions has passed zero-update and readout preparation, and its fixed fit is running; it has no complete TRAIN result yet. Neither the prepared native interfaces nor this working description establishes native accuracy, independent generalization or novelty. The [closed A39 protocol](../reports/a39_null_anchored_set_protocol.md), [A40 result](../reports/a40_role_evidence_context_result.md) and [A41 protocol](../reports/a41_emission_residual_protocol.md) retain their original scientific conditions and stopping rules.

## Representation and candidate generation

An oriented window contains at most 24,576 bases. The fixed grid uses a 12,288-base stride, terminal anchoring and both orientations; padding is not part of the genomic coordinate range. The frozen encoder is the released GENERanno-eukaryote-1.2b-cds-annotator-preview at revision b0483c23b6b63787b61a6d3a204a9b517d6ba345. Four 6,144-base encoding blocks supply aligned six-mer features with 2,048 channels. The existing materialization uses BF16 encoder computation followed by FP32 features; local trainable modules operate in FP32. The annotation-specialized upstream checkpoint is not an audited exposure-free or purely unsupervised base model. Its genome, annotation and validation exposure remains unknown, as documented in [the asset exposure record](../../../paper/supplement/pretrained_exposure.md).

The fixed proposal model has 649,457 parameters and uses its final registered 4,608-update checkpoint. Its unchanged greedy and beam search, with beam four, completed-history retention four and the original candidate cap of 128, define the natural candidate pool under the existing ORF, intron and coding-phase rules. Candidate generation is discrete and outside autograd. It is not updated by the new scorer and does not inject reference chains. Proposal scores and decoder sources describe generation, not the new selection gain.

Training reuses the already closed natural pool and cached encoder features. Conditional native inference instead encodes raw DNA once per oriented window and shares those frozen encoder features and the DNA input between the proposal model and scorer. Each model computes its own local context. Reference annotations, labels and feature caches are not inputs to the native caller.

## Joint context and ordered chain scoring in A39

The scorer is freshly initialized and has 612,324 trainable parameters: 364,256 in SharedContext and 248,068 in JunctionChainHead. SharedContext projects each six-mer vector to 128 dimensions and applies a bidirectional GRU with 64 units per direction. Packed sequence lengths exclude padded tokens. The resulting token context is repeated over six bases and fused with a 32-channel DNA branch: two one-dimensional convolutions with kernel sizes nine and five, each followed by GELU. A linear layer, GELU and layer normalization produce a 128-dimensional base representation. Padded base positions are masked, and all chain features use the valid oriented length.

For an exon [a,b), the head concatenates the mean base representation within the exon, the representations at a and b−1, and three geometry values: log(1+exon length), log(1+preceding gap) and preceding coding length modulo three divided by two. The first exon has gap zero. A linear layer and GELU produce an exon token. For each intervening gap, a junction token uses the intron mean, the preceding exon terminal base, the first and last intronic bases, the next exon initial base, log(1+gap length) and the same preceding coding-phase value. The implementation supports a zero-length gap with zero intronic features; this is not a claim that the natural generator produces biological zero-length introns.

Exon and junction tokens are interleaved in transcript order and passed through a 128-unit GRU. Independent linear readouts sum exon and junction contributions, while a linear–tanh–linear readout of the final recurrent state supplies the nonadditive chain term. A window null energy is a linear readout of the mean valid base representation. For candidate i, the gain is g_i = exon_additive_i + junction_additive_i + nonadditive_i − null. Original proposal, segmentation and query scores are not added to this gain. All candidates in a window share the null energy.

Sources: [SharedContext](../../../pipeline/m28/src/m28/core.py), [ContextChain](../src/m28/context_chain.py) and [JunctionChainHead](../../../pipeline/m28/src/m28/junction_chain.py).

## Candidate role evidence in A40

A40 retains the same context construction, interleaved exon–junction sequence, seven head branches and gain definition. A separate frozen source is the proposal model's original SharedContext and 15-class segmentation branch at step 4,608. For candidate i, the existing role map r_i(b) assigns coding phase, intronic phase, donor, acceptor, start and stop roles from its oriented exon coordinates and accumulated coding length, with the original boundary precedence. It uses no reference chain or candidate label. At each valid base b, the evidence is m_i(b) = source_logit(b,r_i(b)) − source_logit(b,intergenic). This equals the log probability ratio under the same softmax and does not require an additional normalization or fitted offset.

Each exon token receives three additional scalars: the mean role margin over its bases and the margins at its first and last bases. Each junction token receives five: the mean over the intron, the preceding exon terminal margin, the first and last intronic margins, and the next exon initial margin. For a zero-length gap, the three intronic values are exactly zero while both exon endpoint values remain. These scalars enter the respective linear–GELU token projections before the ordered GRU; they are not a separately added segmentation score, a mixture of old scorer outputs or an updated local emission model. The window null continues to use only the student's context.

The fresh student has 613,348 trainable parameters: 364,256 in SharedContext and 249,092 in RoleConditionedChainHead. The extra 1,024 input weights relative to A39 alter subsequent draws in the initialization stream, so the two heads are not tensor-matched initializations. The student loads no old supervised scorer weights, but its 649,457-parameter frozen source has prior supervised training exposure. No gradient or optimizer update reaches that source or the upstream encoder. A40 therefore is neither a complete model trained from scratch nor a matched-capacity and matched-exposure causal ablation.

Sources: [role-conditioned context and head](../src/m28/role_evidence_context.py), [original candidate role map](../../../pipeline/m28/src/m28/phase_evidence_chain.py) and [fixed A40 fit](../scripts/fit_role_evidence_context.py).

## Absolute null and compatible set objective

Let K contain the original known candidate labels 0 and 1, N=|K|, and P contain candidates labelled 1. Label 0 means nonmatching under the primary-reference policy, not an independently established biological negative. Protected label −1 candidates do not enter either supervision term or the structured partition. They remain available during prediction.

Let C contain every subset of K whose half-open chain spans are nonoverlapping, including the empty subset. Its partition is Z_C = Σ_{S∈C} exp(Σ_{i∈S} g_i). The original label policy and implementation require P to be compatible under this same span rule. For N>0, the losses are L_BCE = [Σ_{i∈K} softplus(g_i) − Σ_{i∈P} g_i]/N and L_set = [log Z_C − Σ_{i∈P} g_i]/N. The fixed objective is L_joint = (L_BCE + L_set)/2. Both terms use the same original known-candidate denominator, without class balancing, margins, temperature changes or coefficient searches.

The partition is computed exactly by the sum-product counterpart of weighted interval scheduling. Candidates are sorted by span end; if j(k) is the number of preceding candidates ending at or before candidate k starts, the log-space recurrence is D_k = logaddexp(D_{k−1}, D_{j(k)} + g_k), with D_0=0. This permits multiple compatible genes in one window and is not a softmax choosing a single gene. It is exact for the supplied finite pool and span constraint, not an unrestricted search over all genomic structures.

For an empty or all-protected pool, the original implementation returns differentiable zero and retains the draw in the optimizer sequence. A no-positive window still supervises its known label-0 candidates. Missing reference chains are not inserted into the candidate pool. Protected candidates have zero direct gain derivative, but shared context and null parameters can change their scores indirectly.

For N>0 at fixed gains, the structured distribution is the independent Bernoulli candidate distribution conditioned on compatibility. Consequently L_joint = L_BCE + log Pr_independent(C)/(2N). For each known candidate, its structured occupancy q_i is at most p_i=sigmoid(g_i), and the joint direct gain derivative is [(p_i+q_i)/2−y_i]/N. Thus the joint objective gives no stronger direct label-0 suppression and no weaker direct label-1 promotion than BCE at the same gains. It is not a positive illegal-set-mass penalty. This scalar relationship does not determine shared-parameter AdamW updates or annotation performance; the analytic derivation and closed-score diagnostics are recorded separately in [the gradient mechanism record](../reports/a39_loss_gradient_mechanism.md).

Sources: [mean-known BCE](../../../pipeline/m28/src/m28/core.py), [compatible-set loss](../src/m28/chain_set.py) and [fixed joint objective](../src/m28/null_anchored_chain_set.py).

## Training population and optimization

The training design retains three allowed chromosomes from each of Arabidopsis and rice, 1,491 unique oriented windows and the original 1,536 sampled draws per pass. The natural pool contains 16,321 candidates: 2,380 known positives, 12,566 known nonmatches and 1,375 protected candidates. These are window–candidate observations, not independent genes. Replacement draws and repeated passes do not create independent replicates. The 72 original windows with reference chains missing from the natural pool are neither removed nor augmented with injected chains.

Each arm specifies one seed-zero initialization stream, constructing SharedContext before its respective chain head without loading old supervised scorer weights. It repeats the original draw order and importance weights for three passes, or 4,608 optimizer steps, using AdamW with learning rate 3×10⁻⁴, weight decay 0.01, betas (0.9,0.999), epsilon 10⁻⁸ and gradient-norm clipping at one. Each draw's importance weight multiplies the joint mean-known loss. The frozen encoder features and DNA inputs are detached. No new backbone forward pass, proposal search or relabelling occurs in either fit. A40 additionally computes the frozen source's local segmentation logits from the original cached features and DNA, without query decoding or source updates.

The fit retains pass-end checkpoints at steps 1,536, 3,072 and 4,608, but only the final checkpoint is the main readout; neither seed nor epoch is selected using DEV. Parameter-specific optimizer exposure must be verified rather than assuming every branch receives 4,608 updates. The complete deployed pipeline would retain the proposal model's training exposure in addition to the new scorer fit and unknown upstream encoder exposure. The new scorer's update count is not the total training exposure of the complete method.

Sources: [fixed A39 fit](../scripts/fit_null_anchored_set.py) and [fixed A40 fit](../scripts/fit_role_evidence_context.py).

## Label assisted bounds on the fixed TRAIN pool

A separate exploratory diagnostic measured the geometric selection capacity of the closed A31_A3 natural TRAIN pool. It reused saved candidate geometry, original scores and labels, and sampled-draw identities without generating candidates, loading model or feature tensors, or opening a reference catalogue. The diagnostic required matching, completed source CPU receipts and reproduced the original score-based selections, ordered population, label counts and full reference-window denominators. Counts were summed over the original unique oriented windows, not replacement draws, repeated optimization passes or deduplicated genes. Bounds from a DEV free or owner pool were not substituted for this TRAIN population.

For a window containing T candidates, label-1 candidates received diagnostic gains T+1 plus a unit bonus if they contained at least five exons; label-0 and protected label−1 candidates received zero. The original half-open span interval scheduler then maximized correct-candidate cardinality and, among sets of that cardinality, long-chain cardinality: T+1 exceeds every possible difference in long-chain bonuses. A second assignment gave unit gain only to label-1 candidates with at least five exons, yielding their separate maximum compatible count. All candidate indices were retained in both assignments. These label-assisted gains were diagnostic values, not updates to saved model scores, the fitting objective, the native gain threshold or protected-candidate handling during prediction.

Separate maximum counts do not establish joint feasibility of the continuation conditions. A collection of constructed per-window maximum-correct sets meeting all five conditions provides an arbitrary-score geometric witness, not an actual model PASS. An individual maximum below its necessary continuation count rules out satisfying all five conditions on that fixed pool; sufficient individual maxima without a constructed joint witness leave joint feasibility unresolved. The diagnostic neither establishes that the neural scorer can learn the witness nor measures biological precision, native gene F1, mechanism causality or independent generalization. In particular, zero selected nonmatches under label-assisted gains are not evidence of zero biological false positives.

Sources: [fixed TRAIN bound implementation](../scripts/diagnose_natural_train_selector_bound.py), [diagnostic protocol](../reports/a31_score_pool_train_diagnostic_protocol.md) and [closed result and interpretation](../reports/a31_score_pool_train_diagnostic_result.md).

## Development continuation and conditional native evaluation

The completed A39 readout and CPU reconciliation retained all 1,491 windows and 16,321 natural candidates. It selected 1,496 correct Arabidopsis and 478 correct rice window–candidate pairs, with 131 and 101 known nonmatches; pooled correct chains with at least five exons numbered 653. The two nonmatch conditions passed, but Arabidopsis correctness, strictly increased rice correctness and long-chain correctness failed. A39 closed without native DEV execution or post-hoc changes to its objective, threshold, checkpoint or exposure. These counts are not deduplicated native gene F1.

A40 must close all 1,491 windows and 16,321 natural candidate scores before joining the original saved labels. Separate CPU processing must then reconcile all 4,608 draws and importance weights, the fixed two-term objective, three student checkpoints and branch optimizer exposure, equality of three saved frozen-source states to the original A3 checkpoint and exclusion from the student optimizer, actual saved role inputs and original A31_A3 source scores, FP32 score components and candidate identities, and original span selection with full reference denominators. The readout is configured to save 1,491 local-logit tensors as provenance receipts, not an additional fitting cache. Saved-component reconciliation is not independent neural-forward replay.

Native DEV access requires all five original continuation conditions against the stronger same-pool baseline: Arabidopsis correct window–candidate pairs strictly above 1,531; rice strictly above 478; known-nonmatch selections at most 347 and 251, respectively; and pooled correct chains with at least five exons strictly above 700. The 2,916 eligible reference-window pairs and 1,086 long-chain pairs remain unchanged. Any failed condition closes the arm without changing coefficients, labels, gain threshold, checkpoint or training exposure. Passing A40 would support separately fixing its complete raw-FASTA execution protocol, not automatically execute DEV or establish superiority or publication readiness. The A39 conditional native protocol does not authorize an A40 run.

The prepared A40 native caller scores raw FASTA without labels, but has not been executed on DEV. It would encode GEN once per oriented window and compute the frozen A3 context once, using the same source representation for natural proposals and segmentation logits; the fresh student computes its own context. The conditional DEV grid contains the original two chromosomes, four strand scopes and 8,690 oriented windows. A candidate is owned by its span midpoint under the existing window rule; identical chains are deduplicated, and same-strand weighted interval scheduling maximizes positive total gain at the fixed null threshold zero. Reverse-oriented coordinates are converted back by the existing assembly implementation. The caller emits GFF3, including CDS phase, and preserves candidate identities, sources, nonpositive gains and score components for reconciliation.

The planned evaluation closes the complete predicted grid, assembly and GFF records before opening references. It retains all 7,728 primary CDS-chain references and the original alternative, unsupported and background policies. It reports per-species exact-chain precision, recall and F1, pooled counts, macro and worst-species F1, CDS-base measures, reference-relative background burden, long-chain strata and free-to-owner-to-selected attrition. References are not removed because the proposal model cannot generate them. Prepared interfaces or a successful TRAIN continuation condition cannot replace the actual native result.

## Relation to existing sequence only gene annotation

Neural sequence context and biological-state decoding are established approaches. Tiberius combines a CNN, a bidirectional LSTM and a differentiable HMM in an end-to-end gene predictor. Its April 2026 extension supplies multiple clades, including angiosperms; broader applicability cannot be argued by treating current Tiberius as mammalian-only. The original method and the current official clade support are distinct evidence. [Tiberius original paper](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/40/12/btae685/7903281), [official implementation and clade models](https://github.com/Gaius-Augustus/Tiberius).

Helixer predicts base-wise genic states and coding phase, then decodes primary gene models with an HMM. Its released models include land plants, fungi, vertebrates and invertebrates. Merely using phase evidence, neural context or a discrete structural decoder is therefore not a new task contribution. [Helixer paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02939-1), [official caller and released models](https://github.com/usadellab/Helixer/blob/main/README.md).

ANNEVO's published abstract describes a mixture-of-experts genomic language model for distal sequence dependencies and evolutionary relationships. Its public caller predicts per-nucleotide probabilities and then decodes gene structures. A genomic language model and long-range sequence context alone cannot distinguish the present approach. This description is limited to the published abstract and public caller; it does not establish the absence of other candidate-level mechanisms in ANNEVO. [ANNEVO paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-026-03036-7), [official two-stage caller](https://github.com/xjtu-omics/ANNEVO).

The GENATATORs preprint, posted 21 June 2026, investigates fine-tuned GENA-LM and Caduceus models for gene segmentation. Its gene-level rule accepts an exact match to any annotated isoform, and its combined score includes protein-coding structure and lncRNA reconstruction. That endpoint is not interchangeable with this project's primary-CDS-chain precision and recall on the full genomic grid. [GENATATORs preprint, sections 3.1 and 4](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.06.17.732686).

The released Caduceus segmentation model was trained on gene sequences without intergenic regions, according to its model card. However, the current author pipeline accepts raw FASTA, discovers transcript intervals using boundary and intragenic models, assigns transcript type, segments internal structure and emits GFF3. Thus GENATATOR cannot be dismissed as a segmentation-only comparator, and candidate discovery followed by structural prediction is not by itself a distinguishing contribution. [Segmentation model and training scope](https://huggingface.co/AIRI-Institute/genatator-caduceus-ps-multispecies-segmentation), [complete author pipeline](https://huggingface.co/AIRI-Institute/genatator-pipeline).

A prospective GENATATOR comparison would require the complete pipeline, frozen code and all stage-weight identities, documented training exposure, and the same full-grid primary-CDS and background policies. UTR and lncRNA coverage would be separate endpoints, not pooled into a claim of better CDS-chain annotation. This literature alignment does not change the A40 TRAIN continuation rules or the three fixed external baseline runs; no GENATATOR weights or predictions have been accessed for this project.

The present testable hypothesis is that candidate-conditioned local role evidence, introduced before nonlinear token projection and ordered complete-chain aggregation, improves selection of correct complete CDS chains without increasing reference-relative nonmatches. This is an inference about the experimental design, not an established novelty or superiority claim. Any contribution requires the complete native comparison, mechanism controls with stated capacity and exposure differences, and independently qualified generalization. The comparison uses the [fixed native assets](../../../benchmarks/native_baselines/reports/native_baseline_result.md) and [their exposure record](../../../paper/supplement/pretrained_exposure.md), not interchangeable paper versions or moving latest weights. No baseline weights or runs were changed by this literature alignment.

## Interpretation and independent validation

The A39 objective comparison used a matched architecture and single-seed initialization recipe, but did not provide replicated causal evidence. A40 changes the head's input dimensions and exposes it to a previously supervised frozen local source, so that comparison has additional capacity, initialization and exposure differences. Comparison to the earlier same-pool native caller also changes scorer capacity and supervised training exposure. The full pipeline has two separately trained local models, not an ensemble of the BCE-only and set-only scorers. No claim of matched capacity or matched total exposure against the earlier native caller, or superior runtime, follows from this design.

The Arabidopsis and rice development chromosomes are coordinate-disjoint from their training chromosomes, not species-disjoint or proven homology-isolated. They have already supported repeated method development. Each external baseline has its own fixed weight identity and training-exposure qualifications; raw-FASTA input does not imply that all models are target-species-unseen. Independent species qualification, homolog isolation, a frozen final model and an evaluation policy selected before inspecting predictions remain separate requirements. The [candidate transfer record](../reports/independent_transfer_candidates.md) does not authorize access to new genomes, sealed Setaria or public claims. Learned recurrence, BCE and structured likelihood are established components; a task-specific contribution still requires competitive complete-chain annotation and defensible independent evidence.
