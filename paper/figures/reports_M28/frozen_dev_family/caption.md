# Complete CDS chain accuracy on the fixed DEV panel

## Complete chain F1

The [F1 figure](render_r1/dev_family_complete_chain.svg) retains the complete original eight-method family and separately appends both predeclared extensions. Panels A and B show Arabidopsis thaliana NC_003074.8 and Oryza sativa NC_089041.1, with 5,437 and 2,291 primary references; panel C pools the two contigs and all 7,728 references. The original eight methods appear above the dashed line and the fresh-sampling and joint-encoding extensions below it. Method order follows the fixed family, not performance rank. Filled blue bars identify original model branches, gray bars the three native comparators, and open blue-hatched bars the two extensions.

F1 is `100 × 2TP / (unique predicted chains + primary references)` within each scope. Pooled F1 is calculated from pooled counts, not from the average species F1. Matching requires the sequence identifier, strand and all ordered CDS intervals to agree; phase consistency is assessed separately. Unsupported, overlapping and long-span references remain in the denominator, and other eligible isoform hits do not count as primary TP. All model continuations use their fixed final 9,216-update weights, the initial model its fixed final 4,608 weights, and native tools their original operating points. Labels are rounded to one decimal; [provenance.json](render_r1/provenance.json) retains unrounded counts, precision, recall and F1 for all 30 method-by-scope rows.

Joint 24,576-base encoding exceeded Helixer's complete-chain F1 in both species and pooled, but remained below ANNEVO and Tiberius. Native CDS priors exceeded Helixer pooled and in Arabidopsis but not in rice or on the species macro endpoint. Fresh sampling remained below Helixer in both species. The combined and complete-core TRAIN branches were not added to this DEV family. These are single-fit measurements on historically reused development chromosomes, not independent generalization, a matched upstream-exposure or training-budget comparison, causal mechanism, novelty or checkpoint selection.

## Complete chain precision and recall

The [precision and recall figure](render_r1/dev_family_precision_recall.svg) preserves the same ten methods, scopes, retained references and 0–100% axes. Filled blue bars show `100 × TP / unique predicted chains`; open hatched bars show `100 × TP / primary references`. The paired bars describe two endpoints at each fixed operating point, not a threshold sweep or precision-recall curve. These two figures use the same count rows.

Joint encoding recovered 5,070 primary references among 7,492 predictions, whereas Helixer recovered 5,112 among 8,539. Joint encoding therefore had 42 fewer pooled true positives and 1,047 fewer predictions, 7.806-percentage-point higher precision and 0.543-point lower recall, while its F1 was 3.772 points higher. It recovered 92 more references in Arabidopsis but 134 fewer in rice. Higher F1 does not imply higher recall in both species or uniform superiority across all endpoints. Wholly background chains and CDS-base coverage remain adjacent measurements in provenance and the Results draft, rather than substitutes for full-chain accuracy.
