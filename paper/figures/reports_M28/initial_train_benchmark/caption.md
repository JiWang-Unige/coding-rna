# Initial TRAIN complete CDS chain benchmark

Complete primary CDS-chain F1 for the initial C0 model at its fixed final 4,608-update checkpoint and the three native comparators at their fixed operating points. All four methods annotate the same six raw FASTA contigs. Panels A and B show three contigs from Arabidopsis thaliana and Oryza sativa, with 17,739 and 4,949 primary reference chains, respectively. Panel C pools all six contigs and 22,688 reference chains. Each bar is computed as `100 × 2TP / (predicted + reference)` from the corresponding counts, not by averaging chromosome or species F1. Labels are rounded to one decimal place; unrounded values and TP, predicted and reference counts are retained in [provenance.json](provenance.json).

Matching requires the sequence identifier, strand and every CDS interval to agree; phase consistency is assessed separately. The reference policy selects the longest CDS-assessable primary per gene, breaking ties by transcript ID, and retains difficult and structurally unsupported references. Other assessable isoform matches do not count as primary TP. The initial C0 model has lower complete-chain F1 than all three native tools in both species. This is a TRAIN diagnosis, not an independent generalization result, a matched training-budget comparison or a causal explanation of the deficit. Upstream annotation exposure remains model-dependent. Ongoing continuation and sampling branches are not shown.
