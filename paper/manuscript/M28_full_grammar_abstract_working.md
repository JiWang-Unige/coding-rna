# Complete CDS chain annotation Abstract working draft

2026-10-13 (Asia/Shanghai) evidence snapshot. Based on completed fitting and historically reused development comparisons, including all three fixed fresh-joint controls. Native Carbon-A and both completed encoder-adaptation contrasts remain TRAIN-only; neither adaptation was added to DEV.

Sequence-only protein-coding annotation requires reconstruction of complete CDS chains, not coding-base detection alone. We investigate complete-structure learning under partially resolved supervision. A frozen annotation-pretrained DNA encoder and trainable role-scoring head optimize finite-view likelihoods over compatible gene sets, including the empty set. Fine labels restrict latent roles, coarse region labels enter through an observation channel, and unknown positions are marginalized rather than labelled as background. At inference, overlapping neural-window scores support whole-oriented-contig decoding under literal DNA and reading-frame constraints, without reference-informed candidate enumeration. On two historically reused Arabidopsis and rice development chromosomes containing 7,728 primary reference chains, joint encoding reached pooled exact-chain F1 of 66.623%, versus 54.751% for matched structured continuation. A separate fixed family retained fresh-joint, uniform-coarse and codon-energy final weights regardless of TRAIN ranking, reaching pooled F1 of 70.107%, 69.795% and 64.579%, respectively. All three exceeded fixed Helixer's F1 of 62.851% in both species but remained below Tiberius at 71.805% and ANNEVO at 75.703%. The coarse-channel contrast differed by species, and the codon intervention lowered complete-chain F1 relative to its matched control. In a matched six-chromosome TRAIN replay of the earlier joint-encoding head, 5,067 of 7,762 missed references were individually representable and had positive energy relative to background, but were outscored by selected conflict sets. This descriptive result separates score competition from structural exclusion without establishing a causal repair. Single-fit measurements, reused development data and model-dependent upstream annotation exposure limit interpretation: the current evidence demonstrates method improvement within the evaluated setting, not independently validated superiority to the strongest comparators or broader applicability.
