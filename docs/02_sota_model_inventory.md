## Model families

### Structured neural gene callers
- Members: Tiberius, Helixer, ANNEVO.
- Core idea: neural sequence model produces nucleotide/hidden-state evidence, then a grammar-aware HMM/Viterbi-style layer enforces valid gene structures.
- Strength: best fit to this project because the task asks for base-wise labels plus low intergenic FP and gene-level consistency.
- Weakness: published benchmarks use different species pools, label collapses, and metric definitions; release drift is common.
- Borrow: HMM states, phase/splice/start/stop constraints, gffcompare-style exon/locus metrics, species-level splits.
- Do not borrow blindly: BUSCO-only success, clade-specific leakage, current-release numbers as published claims.

### Foundation / semantic segmentation models
- Members: SegmentNT, GENERanno preview.
- Core idea: large DNA model plus token/base-level segmentation or CDS annotation head.
- Strength: strong representation and CDS/splice/gene-body signal probes; useful backbone source.
- Weakness: public outputs are not guaranteed to be valid gene models; metric and label schema often not comparable to full gene callers.
- Borrow: pretrained embeddings or logits as auxiliary tracks; long-context training recipes; base-wise label heads.
- Do not borrow blindly: claim against Tiberius/Helixer/ANNEVO without a full gene-model decoder and comparable evaluation.
