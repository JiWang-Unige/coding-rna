## Finding (Research) 2026-06-11 — Intergenic ruler choice REVERSES the decoder verdict
The intergenic region MUST be the complement of the FULL-transcript span (incl UTR), not CDS-only — a predicted-genic base in a true UTR is genic, not an intergenic false positive. Re-scoring the same-budget screen ladder under the corrected ruler FLIPS the ranking: the learned CRF-vec decoder (old-ruler winner on gene-body F1, 0.6186) becomes the WORST on the corrected primary intergenic_specificity (0.7138, highest FPR 0.2862), while the plain per-base tiberius_like reference is best (0.8710). Mechanism: structured decoders (CRF/Viterbi) raise gene-body recall/F1 by extending predictions, which SPILLS into intergenic DNA → worse specificity. IMPLICATION: closing intergenic FP without losing recall is a FEATURE/EMISSION problem (foundation probe: SegmentNT/GENERanno), not a decoder problem; structured decoders need an FP-aware objective + better emissions, not just scaling. Validates the user's "change the ruler before the architecture" instinct — CRF-vec Track-B scale-up was paused before any GPU was spent.

## Finding (Research) 2026-06-11 — Pure intergenic_specificity is gameable; dual co-primary needed
On the identical test subset, the ORF FLOOR heuristic has the HIGHEST intergenic_specificity (0.8805, above the trained anchor 0.8710) but the lowest gene_body_F1 (0.3735) — a model that barely annotates anything trivially wins on specificity. So intergenic_specificity alone cannot be the sole primary. Adopted DUAL CO-PRIMARY (Pareto): elevate intergenic_specificity to the headline/ranking axis (honors the research goal of cross-species intergenic stability) BUT require gene_body_F1 >= floor (anti-gaming recall guard) + macro_specificity gate (cross-species stability). This also keeps gene-level F1 as the SOTA-comparable, publishable claim axis (Tiberius/Helixer/ANNEVO headline on F1, not specificity). Both gaming modes blocked: FLOOR by the F1 floor, CRF-vec by the specificity anchor.

## Finding (Engineering) 2026-06-11 — same-budget anchor measured on low-UTR outliers is provisional
yeast+fly are gene-dense, low-UTR/intron species: the UTR ruler-fix is nearly a no-op there (yeast ref_full_transcript 1.755M vs CDS 1.744M, ~0.7% diff) and true_intergenic_bases is small → FPR denominator noisy.
