C2 · Learning from incomplete annotation

On frozen Carbon-A features, marginalising the unlabelled genes avoided the recall collapse of fill-background (+16.5 / +14.2) but gained nothing

Report 14 §2–4 (protocol 13, fixed 17 Oct) · set B, scored once · head-only adaptation · gene-unit bootstrap · one seed

12

What M30 asked (17–19 October, right after the pivot away from the accuracy race). On a new species with only part of its genes annotated, does adapting the grammar layer on top of frozen Carbon-A features beat Carbon-A itself, and is marginalising the unlabelled genes better than calling them background? Protocol 13 was written before any new-species output. Training used other chromosomes of the same two set-B species; the B chromosomes were scored once. Arms. The gate is our grammar decoding Carbon-A’s own frozen per-base outputs with no adaptation. U and F adapt the head with 25% of the species’ genes labelled; U marks the other genes’ loci as unknown, F calls them background. k = 100% adapts with every gene labelled. The k = 10% arms were cancelled by decision. Criteria and verdicts (report 14 §4). - Direction, main endpoint: k = 25% U minus filtered Carbon-A ≥ +3.0 in both species. Result −2.32 [−2.72, −1.94] and −2.73 [−3.17, −2.36]. Not met. - Direction, secondary: U with Carbon-A’s confidence filter minus filtered Carbon-A ≥ +3.0. Result −0.53 and −0.79. Not met. - Mechanism: U − F ≥ +2.0, same sign in both species. Result +16.50 [+15.65, +17.37] and +14.15 [+13.47, +14.82]. Met. What happened. F collapses recall: 44.4% and 46.6% of reference genes recovered, against 71.4% and 68.7% for U, because a quarter-labelled genome teaches that most genes are background. But U is no better than not adapting at all: U − gate = −0.32 [−0.69, +0.05] and −0.79 [−1.19, −0.39]. Even full labels (k = 100%) are 2.40 and 3.16 points below the gate. A 366k-parameter head on frozen Carbon-A features has nothing new to learn; the M29 runs on Arabidopsis and rice had already suggested this. What this left us with on 19 October. The second layer of C2 (marginalise beats fill) held; the first layer (adaptation beats Carbon-A) did not. Because the features were frozen and annotation-supervised, it was unclear whether marginalisation helps when the backbone can actually learn. That question became P4 on the PlantCAD2 backbone (the P4 slides), where it held as well. Yardsticks. Set B, scored once per arm. CIs from the gene-unit + 100 kb orphan-bin bootstrap (2,000 draws). Each arm trained once. ANNEVO and Helixer were not run on set B (no environment on ont).
