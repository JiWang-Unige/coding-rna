Why this matters. Every number in the deck is an upper bound for some reason; this slide says which. Facts from the data verification (topic B), each traced to a file. DEV: Arabidopsis NC_003074.8 (23.5 Mb) and rice NC_089041.1 (29.9 Mb), 7,728 reference genes (5,437 + 2,291), one primary CDS-assessable transcript per gene (the longest CDS). Both species are in PlantCAD2’s pretraining genomes, in Carbon-A’s training snapshot and in Helixer’s training. Our models train on other chromosomes of the same species. DEV has been evaluated once per final model since M31 (M31 A and B, M32, M35, T2, T10, k50U, k50F); before that, dozens of M28-era experiments used it. The planning count of “9 evaluations” in plan 19 is out of date. So DEV ranks models; it is not a test. Set B: Dryas octopetala NC_141367/69/71/73.1 (102.8 Mb, 9,508 genes) and Eschscholzia californica NW_028238309/461/483.1 (170.5 Mb, 12,367 genes). “Time-held-out” means that the first RefSeq annotation of these assemblies (24 and 30 July 2026) appeared after Carbon-A’s weights were public (10 February 2026); it is not about the genome release date. Absence from Carbon-A’s training is inferred from those dates, since the snapshot date is not disclosed. PlantCAD2’s pretraining has only family- or order-level relatives. Homology to our training proteins: 51.8% and 35.6% of set-B proteins have ≥70% identity to the T10 training set (29.1% and 24.5% for T2). The protocol says each model is scored once, but the set as a whole has now seen 12 models or arms on Dryas and 11 on Eschscholzia, and θ = 6.0 for the panel rule was set after T2’s set B numbers were known. Panel: 12 species prepared (8.6 Gb); the core 10 exclude Chenopodium album (congener in pretraining) and the fern Dipteris: 119 main nuclear sequences, 4.79 Gb. None is in PlantCAD2’s pretraining at species level; Amborella is in Carbon-A’s data with an older assembly (AMTR1.0), so it is “held out” only by assembly version, and the documents disagree on whether it counts in the C1 median. Homology audit (MMseqs2, 464,568 panel proteins against the T10 training set): the share with ≥70% identity ranges from 12.2% (Dipteris) to 73.0% (Zizania), median 34.3%. Not audited against the baselines’ training sets. New finding of this review: the panel’s Dryas and Eschscholzia are whole genomes that contain the set B chromosomes, already scored with T2. Report 25 covers the M30 label exposure but not this; two of ten species are therefore not fresh for T2. New-species regions of M34: the first 8 Mb of the median-length chromosome of each of the 8 added species; 5 of the 8 are in PlantCAD2’s pretraining at species (3) or genus (2) level. Sealed: Setaria viridis (GCF_005286985.2) — only its FASTA was ever touched; its annotation has never been read. It is not unseen for Carbon-A, Tiberius or PlantCAD2, so it is a sealed check, not a held-out species.
