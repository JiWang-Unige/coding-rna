The test. Ten training species with PlantCAD2 + LoRA: Arabidopsis and rice fully labelled, and eight new species with half of their genes labelled. k50U treats the other half's positions as unknown and sums over them; k50F calls them background. Scored on the eight new species' internal-validation regions (5,793 reference chains), each arm at the checkpoint its own rule selects on Arabidopsis + rice (step 3,072 in all four arms). Criteria. Seed 1 (registered before results, plan 19): k50U − k50F ≥ +2.0 pooled, same sign in ≥ 6 of 8. Seed 2 (registered 9 November, before any seed-2 output): the two-seed mean ≥ +2.0 and the per-species mean positive in ≥ 6 of 8. Results. Seed 1: 67.86 − 64.62 = +3.24, 8 of 8 (2 November). Seed 2: 69.03 − 54.06 = +14.97, 8 of 8 (21 November). Mean +9.10, per-species means +4.20 to +15.26, 8 of 8. Holds. What drives the margin. In seed 1, recall: k50U finds 4,023 of the 5,793 reference chains, k50F 3,609. In seed 2 almost all of the margin is k50F_s2 under-calling (recall 43.7% against 62.3% in seed 1); k50U_s2 itself is only 1.2 points above k50U. So the average is driven by one checkpoint; the paper should report the two seeds separately. Against full labels (T10, 69.62, one run): two-seed means k50U −1.18, k50F −10.28. Against no labels at all: T2, which never saw these species, scores 68.84 (three T2 runs: 68.84 / 67.28 / 65.83). This is the second critique on the next slides. On DEV (fully labelled in both arms) fill-background still costs: k50F − k50U = −1.29 and −0.92, both CIs excluding 0. Caveat. Both seeds placed the unknown positions using the complete annotation (which genes were unlabelled is known exactly), so this is the upper-bound setting.
