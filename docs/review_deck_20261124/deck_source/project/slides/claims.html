This slide is the table of contents and the honest scorecard. C1, as registered on 27 October: "the same grammar layer works on several backbones, and with a plant-pretrained backbone it approaches Carbon-A zero-shot on new plants". The first half holds on DEV under a controlled comparison (same head, grammar, sampling): NT-v2 + LoRA 63.61, PlantCAD2 + LoRA 72.22, GENERanno 70.11; final T2 73.88. The second half failed on the panel (median gap −12.60; also below Tiberius by 4.20 and Vipsania ft by 2.36). C2: "partial-observation training (marginalise unknown positions) beats filling them with background". Holds in M30 (Carbon-A features, +16.50 / +14.15) and P4 (PlantCAD2, two seeds +3.24 / +14.97, 8 of 8 species each). Both placed the unknown mask using the complete annotation, so they are an upper-bound setting. M42, the realistic version (old RefSeq release as labels, protein evidence as the unknown mask, new release as truth), stopped at gate 1: U is 2.01 points below the zero-shot model and only beats fill-background on missing-locus recall (+2.22). M36 earlier showed the same with pseudo-labels. C3: "the partition-function model gives calibrated gene posteriors for selective annotation". The ranking part holds on the final model across the panel; the calibration part does not (pooled ECE 0.088 is under 0.10, but five species miss the P ≥ 0.9 bin by more than 0.15, which by the rule written before computing must be stated in the main text). C4: descriptive. The panel gap to Carbon-A is concentrated in long genes: half of the recall gap comes from the 8.4% of genes with CDS span ≥ 10 kb, mostly through splitting. M41, the one grammar change aimed at this, had no detectable effect. What would refute each claim now: C1 backbone part, a backbone comparison where the ranking flips with seeds; C2 upper-bound part, M39 insects (paused) failing P4-I; C3 ranking part, another evaluation where the frozen threshold lowers F1; C4, a long-gene analysis on another truth.
