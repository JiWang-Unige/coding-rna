# Complete gene grammar annotation Methods working draft

2026-10-13 (Asia/Shanghai). This draft describes the implemented complete-gene grammar model, not the earlier finite-candidate reranking models. It defines the neural scores, observation likelihood, sampling correction, predeclared continuation branches and raw-FASTA decoder. Accuracy, mechanism effects and independent generalization must be established by the corresponding completed experiments; they are not consequences of these definitions.

## Task and representation

The model predicts complete protein-coding CDS exon chains from genomic FASTA. It does not predict UTRs or enumerate all alternative isoforms. Each orientation is processed separately, with nonoverlapping gene spans within that orientation; overlapping genes on opposite strands remain possible. FASTA sequences are uppercased and non-ACGT symbols are mapped to N without changing sequence length or coordinates. Internal genomic intervals are zero-based and half-open.

The base model uses the frozen GENERanno-eukaryote-1.2b-cds-annotator-preview checkpoint at revision b0483c23b6b63787b61a6d3a204a9b517d6ba345. A 24,576-base neural window is encoded as four native blocks of at most 6,144 bases. Aligned six-mer representations have 2,048 channels; BF16 encoder outputs are materialized as FP32 inputs to the trainable head. This is an annotation-pretrained checkpoint, not an audited exposure-free unsupervised backbone. Freezing it or omitting its original classifier does not remove upstream annotation exposure. The [asset exposure record](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/pretrained_exposure.md) separates disclosed exposure from unresolved training-window and homology isolation.

Carbon-A's fixed provenance maps its preserved main weights to this revision. Its report places both project assemblies in the training-snapshot reference subset, not the temporal holdout. Disclosed annotation supervision and assembly membership do not identify optimizer windows or establish validation and homology isolation. Strict raw-FASTA inference specifies prediction inputs, not assembly-unseen, species-unseen or homology-isolated evaluation. [Fixed provenance](https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceBio/Carbon-A-1.2B/raw/f6789ee79e6ef397de333577ffec1cb734496620/provenance.json), [report sections 5.1–5.2 and Table S3](https://huggingface.co/buckets/HuggingFaceBio/Carbon-A-Technical-Report/resolve/Carbon-A.pdf).

The base head has 366,191 FP32 parameters. It projects token representations to 128 dimensions and uses a bidirectional GRU with 64 units in each direction. Packed lengths exclude padded tokens. Token outputs are repeated over their six aligned bases and concatenated with a 32-channel DNA branch comprising two convolutions, with kernel sizes nine and five and GELU activations. A linear layer, GELU and layer normalization fuse the branches. A final linear layer emits fifteen unnormalized role scores per valid base. The roles distinguish background, coding phase, intronic phase, donor and acceptor boundary roles, start and stop. Only the head is optimized; the backbone remains frozen. These definitions follow the actual C0 implementation in `src/m28/core.py` and the [first-fit recipe](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_first_fit_contract.md).

## Complete structures and score

Let S(x) be the complete, same-strand, span-compatible gene sets accepted by the implemented DNA automaton for an oriented sequence x. The empty set is included. A gene begins with ATG and terminates with TAA, TAG or TGA within one exon. The automaton retains the actual unfinished coding bases across introns, excludes internal in-frame stops, accepts GT or GC donors followed by AG acceptors, requires at least five intronic bases and permits at most 128 exons per gene. Ambiguous bases cannot complete a coding codon, start or splice literal; they may occur in background or intronic interior. Nonstandard structures outside this universe are limitations of the model, not grounds for removing evaluation references.

For a gene set S, let rS(b) be its role at base b, with background role zero, and let zθ(b,r;x) be the head score. Its energy is the unit-weight sum `Eθ(S;x) = Σb [zθ(b,rS(b);x) − zθ(b,0;x)]`. The empty-set energy is zero. There is no learned null offset, length normalization, candidate-pool score or fitted inference threshold. Actual codon carry and the fixed exon budget belong to the structural state, rather than being inferred from a remainder-only phase label.

All-path training and max-path inference use this same structural universe, but different semirings. The training denominator sums the mass of every permitted complete set; it does not restrict competition to an enumerated proposal pool or a Viterbi winner. Exactness is relative to this explicit grammar and supplied sequence, not to all biologically possible genes. The transitions are defined in `src/m28/full_grammar_boundary_messages.py`.

## Partial observations and training likelihood

Only the original core positions carry training observations. A resolved fine label contributes the indicator that the latent role equals that label. When fine is unknown but a coarse label is known, the observation channel assigns probability 0.9 to a matching region class and 0.05 to each of the other two classes. The classes are background, coding including exonic boundary/start/stop roles, and intronic roles. Fully unknown positions and all positions outside the core contribute one. Fine takes precedence over coarse. The fixed channel parameter ε = 0.1 is neither optimized nor interpreted as an estimated biological annotation error rate.

Define `Q(o|S) = ∏b q_b(o_b|rS(b))`, `Zall = ΣS∈S(x) exp Eθ(S;x)` and `Zobs = ΣS∈S(x) exp Eθ(S;x) Q(o|S)`. The unnormalized observation loss is `Lobs = log Zall − log Zobs`. Unknown positions are marginalized rather than filled with background or a selected reference chain. Soft coarse observations do not eliminate the need for a feasible exact-fine event.

The implementation enforces exact fine observations through an allowed-role mask and adds log-likelihood factors only at coarse-only core positions. Because its energy subtracts the background logit, it restores `Σb∈C log q_b(o_b|0)` after that relative partition calculation, where C is the set of coarse-only core positions. Exact-fine indicators are not part of this background correction. Omitting the correction would change the absolute observation probability. The observation formula is implemented in `src/m28/full_grammar_coarse_channel.py`; original ambiguity and callable-base masks are retained rather than relabelled to increase support.

A supplemental TRAIN label assay joined the unchanged 22,688 primary references to the existing 25,512-window pool. Region concordance was evaluated only for individually supported references fully contained in an existing core; unsupported and geometry-ineligible references remained separately unassessed. Original fine, coarse and unknown counts were replayed for every saved reference-core pair. At original coarse-only rows, four in-memory target constructions retained the original masks, cleared only explicit fine-unknown intervals, removed only alternative transcripts from genes with a primary, or applied both changes. Alternative-only gene groups, DNA and callable masks remained protected. All eight fine-restoration patterns were retained. These diagnostic targets were never used for training or inference, and concordance with the same source annotation does not establish independent biological truth or whole-window observation feasibility. The [coarse-label and mask assay](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_coarse_mask_census.md) defines the contrasts and accounting.

Training views contain the original 24,576-base core and its raw-coordinate stride-neighbor windows on either side, clipped and deduplicated at chromosome boundaries. A base receives all fifteen scores from its nearest containing window center; ties choose the lower genomic start on both strands. Reverse-complement processing changes row coordinates and piece order, not role columns. Interior message boundaries carry all reachable scored states; only the outer edges of the finite DNA view are closed in background. Consequently, this is a collection of finite-view observation likelihoods, not chromosome-joint maximum likelihood, even though inference later decodes whole contigs.

FP64 log-space sum-product messages retain every reachable state. A checkpointed CPU implementation recomputes blocks during reverse-mode differentiation and supplies first-order derivatives through incoming messages and neural logits. Its 256-base checkpoint spacing changes memory use, not structural closure. Exon-budget dominance used by the max decoder is not applied to these partitions: discarding a lower-probability reachable path would change their summed mass. The implementation is `src/m28/full_grammar_boundary_compiled.py`.

## Optimization and sampling

For each draw w, the fitted objective is `a_w Lobs(w) / max(1, F_w + C_w)`, where F_w and C_w count fine and coarse-only core rows and a_w is the specified importance weight. The denominator is known observation rows, not all valid bases, gene count or chain length. A fully unknown draw remains in the update sequence with zero observation loss; AdamW momentum and weight decay can still change parameters.

The first fit used a fresh seed-zero head, 1,536 fixed draws from 1,491 unique windows, three repeated passes and 4,608 updates. AdamW used learning rate 3×10⁻⁴, weight decay 0.01, betas 0.9/0.999, epsilon 10⁻⁸ and gradient-norm clipping at one, with batch size one and no scheduler. Native frozen representations were recomputed per draw; feature caches and old supervised head weights were not model inputs. The formal readout uses the last checkpoint, not the best intermediate loss or DEV score. Continuations restore that checkpoint and its AdamW state for another fixed 4,608 updates; differing objectives, context and native-head-prior branches retain their separate protocols rather than being described as one controlled factor.

For the objective contrast, local role probabilities are `pθ(b,r) = exp zθ(b,r;x) / Σk exp zθ(b,k;x)`, with the sum over all fifteen roles, and the unnormalized local observation loss is `Llocal = −Σb∈core log[Σr pθ(b,r) q_b(o_b|r)]`. The observation factors use the same fine/coarse hierarchy as the structured likelihood: a fine label selects its exact role probability, while a coarse-only label weights matching-region roles by 0.9 and other roles by 0.05 before summing their probabilities. This is a fifteen-role softmax, not a separately normalized three-region classifier. Fully unknown core rows have unit observation likelihood and zero local gradient. The local calculation uses FP64 log-softmax and log-sum-exp, without enforcing literal DNA or complete-chain compatibility in its training likelihood.

The predeclared structured, local and mixed continuation losses are respectively `a_w Lobs / d_w`, `a_w Llocal / d_w` and `a_w (Lobs + Llocal) / (2 d_w)`, where `d_w = max(1, F_w + C_w)`. The original draws, order, observations, importance weights, optimizer settings and starting AdamW moments are unchanged, with the formal readout fixed at cumulative update 9,216. No additional class, boundary, length or null weights are introduced. The mixed coefficients are fixed at 0.5 and do not equalize loss or gradient scales; matching update counts does not establish matched effective update magnitudes. All three readouts retain the same complete-gene grammar and whole-contig raw-FASTA caller, even when the training objective is local. These definitions follow `src/m28/local_coarse_channel.py` and the [objective contrast protocol](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_objective_contrast_contract.md). The completed objective comparison uses these three final readouts without DEV-based selection.

The complete-core sampling branch uses the existing natural TRAIN windows, stratified by species, chromosome and orientation. In a stratum with N windows, k_w is the number of supported primary-reference incidences wholly contained in w with every span role resolved and matching fine supervision, and K = Σw k_w. Its fixed proposal is `q_w = 0.5/N + 0.5 k_w/K`, with replacement, and `a_w = 1/(N q_w)`. All declared strata have K > 0. Every natural and background window retains positive probability, and a_w is at most two. This preserves the within-stratum uniform expectation of the normalized per-window loss before nonlinear optimizer operations; it does not match clipping effects, variance or AdamW trajectories. Eligibility depends on TRAIN geometry and labels, never baseline or model predictions. The [complete-core protocol](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_complete_core_draws_plan.md) fixes the sampling seed and schedule and compares the final 9,216-update model with both fresh-uniform and repeated-structured controls at the same update count.

## Native CDS priors and joint-window encoding

The native-prior branch retained the original head and added a zero-initialized, bias-free linear map from two native CDS-evidence columns to the fourteen nonbackground role increments. For each valid base, each evidence column was the frozen native head's six-position CDS score minus its NON_CODING score, materialized in FP32. If u_h(b) denotes that evidence, the modified role score is `z′(b,r) = z(b,r) + Σh λ[r,h] u_h(b)` for r = 1,…,14; role zero is unchanged. The 28 coefficients were trainable, but both original BF16 classifiers and the encoder remained frozen. The branch had 366,219 trainable parameters, with no auxiliary classifier loss, fitted inference threshold or native-head selection. Native annotation-pretrained scores are reused inputs, not a new model contribution by themselves.

The native-prior continuation restored all original head AdamW moments at step 4,608 and appended a new coefficient group starting at step zero. All parameters used the same optimizer settings and one common gradient-norm clip; after 4,608 additional updates, original parameters reached step 9,216 and new coefficients step 4,608. Original draws, importance, observations and structured likelihood were unchanged. Parameter addition changes gradient flow and shared clipping, so matched schedules do not imply identical effective optimization. The [native-prior protocol](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_native_cds_prior_fit.md) fixes the coefficient and checkpoint identities.

The joint-context branch replaced four separate 6,144-base encoder blocks with one native forward over the same 24,576-base read window, corresponding to 4,096 six-base tokens. It retained the fixed GENERanno revision, bidirectional SDPA flash attention, BF16 hidden states and FP32 head inputs, without changing backbone parameters or rotary embeddings. Short windows were oriented first and right-padded with N; only valid original bases entered the score field and grammar. This branch did not use the native CDS classifiers. The combined branch used this encoding and the same two-head prior map. Both retained the original draws, additional update count and whole-contig decoder; the [joint](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_joint24_fit.md) and [combined](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_joint24_native_prior_plan.md) protocols specify their independent final checkpoints.

The fresh-uniform branch instead used a fixed seed-one schedule of 4,608 draws from 4,219 unique original natural TRAIN windows, with importance one. It kept the original 6,144-base encoding, structured observation loss and optimizer starting state. The complete-core mixture described above used 4,107 unique windows. These sampling contrasts change finite supervision exposure and gradient variation, not just the number of fully covered reference chains. All branches used their prespecified final update-9,216 readout, rather than a checkpoint chosen from TRAIN or DEV performance.

A separate experiment combines the unchanged fresh-uniform schedule with joint 24,576-base encoding to fill the missing sampling-by-encoding cell. It uses the same update-4,608 starting weights and AdamW state, 4,608 additional updates, original observations and final-update readout; the three completed control cells are reused without retraining. The fixed TRAIN interaction is F11−F10−F01+F00 on count-derived complete-chain endpoints, separately for chromosomes, species, pooled counts, species macro and worst species. This design was specified after the complete DEV results had been read, not preregistered before DEV; it is motivated by the incomplete TRAIN factorial comparison and does not use DEV to select parameters or checkpoints. It is not part of the original eight-method or completed ten-method DEV family, and its original-TRAIN accuracy and four-cell comparison are now complete. The [completed four-cell result](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_fresh_joint24_result.md) preserves the observed improvements and negative F1-scale interaction. The [fresh-sampling joint-encoding protocol](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_fresh_joint24_plan.md) retains the fixed schedule, direct four-input DNA comparison and single-fit exploratory scope. Its separately prepared DEV comparison is defined below.

## Complete CDS codon energies

The codon branch augments the role-only energy without changing the accepted structures. Let n_k(S;x) count codon k in the oriented, spliced CDS sequences of S. The 64 raw FP32 coefficients β follow lexicographic ACGT order. For each codon, `β̄_k = β_k − mean_{j∈G(k)} β_j`, where G(k) is either the 61 sense codons or the three stops. Equivalently, β̄ = Pβ for the block-diagonal within-group centering projection P. The augmented energy is `Eθ,β(S;x) = Eθ(S;x) + Σk n_k(S;x) β̄_k`. The empty set retains energy zero, and β = 0 exactly nests the role-only model. Centering leaves 62 coefficient directions; it does not establish that all of them are independently identifiable from the jointly fitted neural scores.

Every complete CDS triplet contributes once, including the initial ATG and the terminal TAA, TAG or TGA. Internal triplets may span introns: the unchanged automaton carries their actual unfinished coding bases and scores a triplet only when its third coding base is consumed. Intronic and background bases receive no codon contribution. Stops split across introns, internal in-frame stops, ambiguous coding bases and incomplete genes remain excluded. The implementation adds codon indices to existing closure edges rather than introducing states or paths; training partitions, MAP decoding and reported chain energies use the same coefficients.

Both Zall and Zobs replace Eθ with Eθ,β while retaining the original fine/coarse observation factors, absolute background correction and known-row normalization. Define `pall(S) = exp Eθ,β(S;x)/Zall` and `pobs(S) = exp Eθ,β(S;x) Q(o|S)/Zobs`. At fixed θ, the unnormalized observation-loss derivative is `∇β Lobs = Pᵀ [Epall n(S;x) − Epobs n(S;x)]`. The fitted per-draw objective multiplies this derivative by `a_w / max(1, F_w + C_w)`. Checkpointed FP64 sum-product differentiation accumulates the codon contributions through all reachable states and through incoming messages; it does not apply MAP dominance pruning. The actual-carry max-only skyline remains valid because dominated states share all future role and codon increments. The implementations are `src/m28/full_grammar_codon.py` and `src/m28/full_grammar_codon_skyline.py`.

The branch starts from the same update-4,608 head and restored AdamW state as fresh-sampling joint encoding. Its 64 coefficients start at zero with new optimizer moments. The unchanged seed-one schedule has 4,608 draws from 4,219 original TRAIN windows, importance one, joint 24,576-base encoding and ε = 0.1. Head and codon parameters share the original AdamW settings and one norm-one gradient clip; the encoder remains frozen and native CDS classifiers are not inputs. The sole formal readout has head optimizer age 9,216 and codon optimizer age 4,608, with 366,255 trainable parameters in total. Adding the coefficients also changes gradient flow and joint clipping, so the paired contrast tests the complete scoring-and-optimization intervention, not an isolated effect of codon composition. The [codon protocol](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_codon_factor_preparation.md) specifies the strict final-checkpoint adapter and original six-chromosome TRAIN comparison. Its artificial-DNA and disposable native checks are implementation evidence, not final-model accuracy.

The coefficients are conditional energy terms, not codon probabilities or empirical codon-usage estimates. Group centering makes the energy invariant to a uniform raw-coefficient shift within either group; it does not make the sum neutral to observed composition, CDS length or gene count. In particular, every gene contributes an ATG start term in addition to any internal ATG terms, and one terminal stop term. Neural and codon energies must therefore be interpreted jointly, with complete-chain recovery and background burden assessed in the fixed paired readout rather than inferred from individual coefficient signs.

## Additional information and encoder adaptation contrasts

Uniform-coarse continuation tests the observation channel during additional learning, not its removal from training since initialization. It restores the same update-4,608 head and AdamW state as fresh-sampling joint encoding and retains the same seed-one order of 4,608 draws from 4,219 original TRAIN windows, joint 24,576-base encoding, importance one and original fine-plus-coarse denominator. Only the additional-fit channel changes from ε = 0.1 to ε = 2/3. Each coarse region then has probability one third for every latent role, so its raw negative log likelihood contributes the parameter-independent constant `C_w log 3`. Fine observations remain hard constraints and unknown positions remain marginalized; coarse targets are not deleted and the denominator is not replaced by a fine-only count. The original head's first 4,608 updates already used informative coarse observations, and upstream annotation exposure is unchanged. The final readout is cumulative update 9,216. The [uniform-coarse protocol](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_uniform_coarse_continuation_plan.md) therefore tests the marginal effect of information in the additional learning phase, not a from-scratch coarse-free model or a universal necessity claim; absolute losses with different observation constants are not compared as accuracy measures.

A separate low-rank encoder-adaptation branch uses the same starting head, ordered draws, observations, joint context and complete-set objective as the fresh-sampling joint control. It attaches rank-four, scale-one residuals to the query and value projections of zero-based encoder layers 22–25, keeping every original encoder weight frozen. For each original BF16 projection output, the residual is computed as `B(A(x_FP32))` in FP32, rounded to BF16 and added without merging into the released weight. Each A tensor has shape 4×2,048; B has shape 2,048×4 for query and 256×4 for value. A is initialized uniformly within ±1/√2,048 using a local CPU generator with seed zero, and B starts at zero. The eight projections contain sixteen FP32 tensors and 102,400 new parameters; together with the original 366,191-parameter head, 468,591 parameters are trained. Encoding retains the native PyTorch SDPA FlashAttention backend and BF16-to-FP32 hidden representation. The encoder stays in evaluation mode without dropout or gradient checkpointing, but gradients pass through the adapters; evaluation mode does not make the adapted representation frozen.

Head AdamW moments continue from age 4,608 to 9,216, while adapter moments start at zero and reach 4,608. All trained parameters share the original optimizer settings and one norm-one gradient clip. The unchanged grammar, empty-set energy, observation channel and raw-FASTA caller receive no extra classifier loss, codon term or fitted threshold. Final checkpoints retain the head, all sixteen named adapters and optimizer state, not another copy of the pretrained backbone; inference restores the fixed base and all adapters before disabling gradients. The [adapter protocol](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_joint_lora_fit.md) fixes the final readout and original six-chromosome comparison. Added capacity, different optimizer ages and resulting joint clipping and optimization trajectories are part of this intervention, not an isolated biological causal effect. This branch remains TRAIN-only and is not added to the separately prepared three-candidate DEV plan or the completed eight- and ten-method families. Disposable implementation and native preflight updates do not establish accuracy; both the coarse-channel and low-rank adaptation TRAIN contrasts have completed. The [completed adaptation result](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_joint_lora_result.md) preserves the improved chain endpoints and increased background burden without changing the frozen DEV family. The [completed coarse-channel result](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_uniform_coarse_result.md) does not change this protocol or select a DEV candidate.

The separately completed post-outcome adaptation replay used the saved 22,688-primary-reference profile and the two unchanged final-output GFFs, without rereading DNA, reference GFFs or checkpoints. Unique keys included species, contig, strand and ordered zero-based half-open CDS intervals; source evaluations had already checked phase and DNA. Reparsed TP, prediction and duplicate counts had to reproduce each completed chromosome evaluation. Retained, gained, lost and still-missed references were partitioned by one, two-to-five or at-least-six CDS segments, and separately by genomic CDS span at most or over 24,576 bases. Both partitions retained every reference and were reconciled against species and pooled totals. These post-outcome reference bins estimate recall intersections, not subset prediction precision/F1, independent repetition or causal attribution.

### Full rank q v continuation and prediction replay

A separate fixed continuation adapted the eight bias-free query and value matrices in encoder layers 22–25 as full FP32 master weights, casting them to the original BF16 dtype at each forward. Their 18,874,368 parameters were trained together with the 366,191-parameter role head; all other encoder weights, RoPE and native attention behavior remained unchanged. The encoder as a whole was therefore adapted rather than frozen. The head resumed the original update-4,608 AdamW state with learning rate 3 × 10⁻⁴, whereas the native matrix group began at age zero with learning rate 10⁻⁵. Both retained the original weight decay, beta values, epsilon and a shared norm-one gradient clip.

The single fit used the same seed-one ordered 4,608 fresh TRAIN draws and joint-24,576-base inputs, observation likelihood, known-row denominator, ownership, cap and FP64 structural computation as the frozen control. Only the predefined final head-update-9,216/native-update-4,608 checkpoint was evaluated. At inference all eight adapted matrices were restored before gradients were disabled, and the unchanged raw-FASTA caller solved each oriented whole contig without references, labels, feature caches or parameter updates. The [fixed protocol](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_dense_qv_plan.md) and [completed result](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_dense_qv_result.md) distinguish this intervention from low-rank adaptation and preserve TRAIN-only scope.

After both outcomes were known, a separate CPU replay compared the two fixed prediction sets against the unchanged saved primary-reference profile. It classified each of the 22,688 references as recovered by both, full-rank only, low-rank only or neither, and reused the existing reference-defined bins of one, two-to-five and at least six CDS segments and span at most or above 24,576 bases. Unique predictions, duplicate counts and primary-hit counts had to reproduce every completed chromosome, species and pooled evaluation. Both source caller-DNA receipts had already matched the same normalized original TRAIN sequences. No DNA, real reference GFF, weights or native model forwards were read in this replay; only existing predictions and the saved reference profile were used. The diagnostic is explicitly post-outcome and exploratory; its bins measure reference recall, not subgroup prediction precision/F1 or an isolated rank effect.

## Raw FASTA decoding

Inference receives only FASTA and the fixed final checkpoint. It does not read annotations, observation-channel targets, feature caches or an injected reference candidate pool. Neural windows use the fixed 24,576/12,288-base width/stride grid, including terminal coverage. The same nearest-containing-center rule produces one fifteen-role row at every oriented contig base, without averaging overlapping predictions. A single complete-set MAP solve is then run for each entire oriented contig, allowing genes to cross neural-window boundaries. Genes are not closed inside individual windows and subsequently filtered by ownership.

The production decoder uses an exact max-only exon-budget skyline. At the same sequence position, stage and actual coding carry, a state with no lower score and fewer used exons dominates a higher-budget state because it retains all of the latter's possible futures. FP64 accumulation and 8,192-base traceback checkpoints retain the score and complete structure. Equal-score choices follow background continuation and the implementation's state-table arrival/lower-budget policy; exact optimal score does not imply identical tied chain identities across different backends. Genomic coordinates and CDS phases are exported as gene, mRNA and CDS GFF3 records and checked against the actual DNA. Implementation and completed input/output verification are linked in the [raw-FASTA contract](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_raw_train_contract.md).

The separate equal-energy caller independently restores the rank-four and full-rank encoders from their fixed final checkpoints and freshly encodes the same input FASTA with each. It promotes their aligned fifteen-role rows to FP64 before averaging with fixed weights 0.5 and 0.5, then applies the same whole-contig MAP decoder. Its runtime reads neither cached arrays nor old predictions, references or labels, and role arrays are not persisted. Two fixed TRAIN views and a short artificial sequence were checked against independent branch reencoding, an independent FP64 averaging expression, full-chain geometry, exported gains and ordered CDS phases. The [native qualification](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_e_m_raw_native.md) establishes this finite-input equivalence without substituting its timings or accuracy for whole-chromosome execution.

The minimum-centered caller separately implements a fixed local disagreement penalty. For each aligned base and role it computes the lower of the two FP64 energies relative to each branch's own background, equivalent to their centered mean minus half their absolute disagreement. This is not the minimum of whole-gene energies or an intersection of emitted chains. It restores the two native models independently and uses the same raw input normalization, context ownership, whole-contig decoder and GFF export; runtime inference does not read saved role arrays or references. The [completed native qualification](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_minimum_centered_raw.md) reused the same two fixed TRAIN views and short sequence as the mean caller. All six oriented outputs matched independent fresh branch reencoding, an independent minimum-energy expression, whole-contig MAP gains, exported geometry and ordered phases. The two nonempty complete chains and four empty outputs were retained, with peak allocated VRAM of 4.536 GiB. This finite-input integration does not substitute for new whole-chromosome native timings or accuracy.

## CDS event marginal support

For fixed oriented DNA x and its unchanged role scores, the normalized structural model is `Pθ(S|x) = exp Eθ(S;x) / Zall(x)`. The denominator includes every complete, same-strand, span-compatible gene set in the specified grammar and the empty set. The readout queries the original MAP CDS chains; these queries do not define a candidate universe or restrict the denominator. No training observation factor `Q(o|S)`, reference annotation, fitted temperature or correctness classifier enters this inference distribution.

For a complete queried CDS chain g occupying the half-open span [a,b), define `αB(a)` as the log mass of complete exterior sets ending in background at a and `βB(b)` as the log mass of complete exterior sets beginning in background at b. Same-strand span exclusion separates the legal exterior structures on either side of g, giving

`log Pθ(g ∈ S | x) = αB(a) + Eθ(g;x) + βB(b) − log Zall(x)`.

Only the gene event constrains its exterior boundary states. The full partition is not reset or closed at internal query points, so competing structures may cross these points when g is absent. For one fixed complete compatible set S0, `log Pθ(S = S0 | x) = Σg∈S0 Eθ(g;x) − log Zall(x)`; the empty-set log probability is `−log Zall(x)`. A single-gene marginal sums over compatible exterior genes and alternative sets. It is therefore not generally the sigmoid of its gain against background, and the product of gene marginals does not generally give the exact-set probability. The two orientations remain independent; no opposite-strand exclusion is introduced.

The implementation in `src/m28/full_grammar_gene_posterior.py` uses two FP64 sum-product scans over the supplied DNA and unchanged actual-codon-carry/exon-budget automaton. It retains rolling state messages and the queried background boundary scalars, without max-only skyline pruning. Whole-contig role arrays are still retained; sparse boundary storage is not a claim that total memory is independent of sequence length. Log probabilities remain available when their exponentials underflow. Forward and reverse total masses must agree, and supported event mass must not exceed the full partition beyond the declared numerical tolerance.

Independent complete-set enumeration and direct role-constrained numerators validated the implementation on artificial sequences and fixed native TRAIN views. This qualification is finite-view mathematical and engineering evidence, not a completed whole-chromosome calibration or performance result. The [qualification record](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_gene_posterior_preflight.md) and [whole-TRAIN readout protocol](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_gene_posterior_train_plan.md) distinguish these scopes. Conditional structured modelling and exact inference have established [semi-Markov CRF precedents](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2004/hash/eb06b9db06012a7a4179b8f3cb5384d3-Abstract.html); deriving an event marginal is not, by itself, an originality claim.

The numerically stabilized message kernels in `src/m28/full_grammar_scaled_mass.py` recenter every 256 consumed bases. Each complete forward or backward state vector is shifted by its current finite background component, and the removed constants are accumulated with compensated addition and restored at every queried boundary and at the global endpoint. Open-gene states are retained across these cuts. This uniform change of representation neither closes internal boundaries nor changes role energies, legal paths, empty-set energy or the event-probability formula. The original forward/reverse mass tolerances and probability guards remain unchanged; no temperature or threshold is fitted.

Eight synthetic checks, including a million-base product of independent literal ORF choices, qualified this representation against enumeration, analytic masses and the established probability tests. A subsequent read-only whole-chromosome qualification on the rank-four Arabidopsis chromosome NC_003070.9 plus orientation obtained identical forward and reverse totals in the reported FP64 representation, 844406.735716842, while preserving all 3,467 original MAP-query gains and legal chains. The completed readout then covered all 24 model–chromosome–orientation combinations and replayed both fixed prediction pools before reference access. It reused the original role arrays, weights, queries and unchanged numerical tolerances. One separately qualified orientation was reused; the other 23 were computed in the readout. The separate missed-reference score assay was completed after reconciling four fully written chromosome blocks and recomputing two chromosomes with the original scientific calculation, preserving the failed attempt and its source provenance. The [numerical qualification and completed readout record](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/full_grammar_gene_posterior_train_plan.md) preserves these distinct outcomes.

Ranking uses the complete fixed prediction pool separately under exact-primary and any-assessable-isoform labels. AP integrates the grouped precision–recall curve and AUROC handles equal scores jointly; every declared fraction (25%, 50%, 75%, 90% and 100%) retains complete ties. Prediction-pool positive recall is kept separate from recall against all original primary references. Fixed probability bins and reference-relative Brier scores describe the two benchmark events without treating structural inclusion as a calibrated probability of biological correctness. No temperature, threshold or operating point is fitted, and the existing MAP GFFs remain unchanged.


Structural inclusion is not the same event as exact agreement with an annotation or biological correctness. The fixed TRAIN diagnostic protocol specifies comparisons of log-marginal and internal-gain rankings for exact-primary and exact-any-assessable-isoform matches among the already emitted predictions. Exact score ties stay grouped; average precision and AUROC use this selected prediction pool, excluding unpredicted references. Primary-reference recall separately retains the full reference denominator. Retained fractions 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.9 and 1.0 are predefined diagnostics, not selected operating points or new filtered GFF outputs. Reference-relative Brier values and ten fixed equal-width probability bins assess mismatch between structural support and these reference events; they do not fit a calibrator, prove biological calibration or identify every unmatched prediction as a false gene. No frozen DEV family is expanded by this TRAIN readout.

## Evaluation and interpretation

Complete-chain matching includes sequence identifier, strand and every CDS interval. CDS phase consistency is checked separately. Evaluation uses the fixed CDS-assessable primary policy, selecting the longest eligible CDS per gene and breaking length ties by transcript ID. It retains references outside the model grammar and does not filter them by training eligibility, motif, exon cap or recoverability. Pooled precision/recall/F1 is calculated from pooled counts; per-species, species-macro and worst-species results remain separate. CDS-base unions, duplicate predictions, other-isoform matches and background burdens supplement rather than replace complete-chain accuracy. Reference-relative background is outside annotated gene-feature spans on either strand, not simply outside CDS bases.

ANNEVO, Tiberius and Helixer are evaluated using their fixed native outputs and operating points under the same reference policy. TRAIN results are fitting/development diagnostics. The existing repeatedly used DEV chromosomes are development comparisons, not new independent tests, and upstream exposure is reported by model rather than collapsed into a universal zero-shot claim. Sealed data remain closed, and completed frozen evaluation families are retained without alteration. ## Relation to prior work

Whole-structure conditional likelihood is established in gene prediction: Conrad trains a semi-Markov CRF by conditional maximum likelihood with forward/backward gradients. Neural representations, latent direction/codon states, likelihood training and Viterbi prediction are also present in GeneDecoder, including experiments with pretrained DNA embeddings. Neither a gene-grammar partition function nor a frozen DNA representation followed by structured decoding is sufficient by itself to establish originality. [Conrad Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1950907/), [GeneDecoder Methods and embedding experiments](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.03377v1).

Sequence-content energies likewise have direct precedents. AUGUSTUS models coding content with interpolated Markov models, reading-frame-dependent components and separate stop-codon probabilities. The earlier Conrad work combines generative features and nonprobabilistic indicators in a discriminatively weighted CRF. The present centered 64-coefficient spliced-CDS term is a specific experiment within this established class of sequence-dependent structural scoring, not a novelty claim based on adding codon scores. [AUGUSTUS original model section](https://gobics.de/mario/papers/GenePred2003.pdf), [Conrad 2006 sections 2 and 3](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2006/file/65a31da7ede4dc9b03fb5bbf8f442ce9-Paper.pdf).

The Carbon-A technical report additionally describes strand-specific coding probabilities, a transition TCN, a sequence-constrained Viterbi gene grammar and a confidence TCN, followed by GFF3 and CDS/protein FASTA output. This is a directly relevant complete-structure precedent, not merely a base-classification model. Its reported pipeline must be distinguished from the narrower released probability-inference examples and from an executed, input-matched baseline. [Carbon-A technical report pages 24 and 25](https://huggingface.co/buckets/HuggingFaceBio/Carbon-A-Technical-Report/resolve/Carbon-A.pdf), [fixed asset and comparison-readiness record](../reports/M28-METHOD-RESTART/carbon_a_pipeline_readiness.md).

The task-specific combination described here—complete-set competition under literal DNA constraints, partially observed fine/coarse labels, base-owned native-window scores and whole-contig output—defines what the experiments test, not a proven first-of-kind algorithm. A paper-level contribution still requires competitive complete-chain performance or independently established applicability, with the corresponding controlled mechanism evidence and computational costs. The [prior-work analysis](M28_complete_chain_competition_prior_work_20261003.md) retains the broader precedents and distinctions. No superiority or independent-generalization claim follows from the implementation alone.
